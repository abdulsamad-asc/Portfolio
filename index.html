About Me

Practical experience, applied where it's needed

I started out on the operations side of computers — running the day-to-day computer work for a school, then moving into hands-on repair, printing and digital services at a local computer shop. Along the way I picked up graphic design and data entry as everyday necessities, and more recently I've been building out my web development skills through structured training and personal projects in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP.

What I bring isn't a single specialism — it's the ability to move between practical computer support, design work and web development without losing attention to detail. If something needs to be set up, designed, entered, filed or built, there's a good chance I've already done something close to it.
